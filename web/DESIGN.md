# PariView design decisions

Current user-approved direction, 2026-11-04

- Keep the letter-free glasses symbol and extruded PariView wordmark using Three.js and Anime.js
- Use the supplied TeamSparta visual vocabulary: white and cool gray, muted mint #257F76 primary actions (hover #1D665F, tint #EAF6F4), #003CDC links, 16px panels and pill buttons
- Use self-hosted Pretendard; Toss Product Sans is not bundled
- Match the observed Toss scale: main headings 36–42px, introduction 16–20px, comfortably readable controls
- Center the opening message within a constrained content width; keep generous white space
- Approved headline: 화면과 소리, / 내가 정하면 안 되나요?
- No sentence-final periods in UI copy; keep numeric decimals and bibliographic identifiers
- No decorative status dots
- No room, pedestal or large glasses model
- Remove the rejected bridge scene, sketch intro and laptop intro from the main page
- Show the hologram excerpt as the actual movie preview
- Required flow: profile selection → visual, auditory and cognitive preference exploration → personalized viewing
- Distinguish research results, design inference and unvalidated product effects
